Dr. Fabián Poletti

Md, MSc (DIC), FRCS (Eng), FEBOT (T&O)

Consultant Orthopaedic and Trauma Surgeon. Knee Surgery. Hip and Knee Arthroplasty. Sports Medicine. Regenerative Medicine and Orthobiologics.

Dr. Fabian Poletti is a Consultant Orthopaedic and Trauma Surgeon based in the beautiful Costa del Sol area (Spain). He visits patients from Marbella, Estepona, Sotogrande and Gibraltar.

Dr Poletti specialises in the treatment of bone and joint diseases. His areas of expertise includes all aspects of knee surgery including arthroscopic (keyhole) surgery: meniscal repairs and ligament (ACL) reconstruction, realignment surgery (osteotomies around the knee) and partial/total joint replacement. Hip and Knee Arthroplasty. Sports Medicine. Regenerative Medicine and Orthobiologics.

Most of his early years were spent training and playing various sports, which included athletics (National schools 100m and 110 m hurdles and long jump) and football (National schools.) During his university years he went on to spend time playing rugby at his hometown most successful club: San Jose Rugby Club, gaining representative honours with the Rugby Team of the Medical School of Asuncion. More recently, he has turned his attention to Master Level Athletics (100m and long jump) and boxing.

With his extensive sporting background, he also has a strong interest in sports injuries and their management including Regenerative Medicine and Orthobiologics (Platelet Rich Plasma/PRP, Hyaluronic Acid, Stem Cells Therapy, etc). Mr Poletti has a particular interest in the "younger" arthritic knee and the evolving "joint preserving" surgical techniques such as meniscal repair, osteotomies around the knee (leg realignment), cartilage transplantations and partial joint replacements.

A special area of interest of Dr Poletti is enhanced recovery after Knee and Hip Arthroplasty Surgery. With his extensive background Mr. Poletti has developed his own soft tissue friendly techniques and rapid recovery protocols that allow patients to recover faster and walk as soon as 2 hours after surgery being ready for discharged between 12 and 36 hours after surgery.

He regularly performed knee and hip arthroplasty surgery in the very obese or the very elderly patients that have been denied surgery elsewhere. Mr Poletti believes that older age and weight should not be a reason to exclude patients from the great benefits of joint replacement surgery. In fact, this benefit has been proved in several scientific studies for this particular group of patients.

In the Knee replacement field Mr Poletti embraced the so called “Personalized Surgery”, keeping up with most newest advances and cutting edge technology in this field including: Fixed bearing partial knee replacement (half knee surgery), Kinematic alignment techniques (physiological alignment), Medial pivot/medial congruent philosophy (cutting edge implants) as well as technology assisted surgery (navigation, augmented reality, etc).

Mr Poletti early medical training (1998-2006) started at University of Asuncion, in 1998 and continued at The Universita’ degli Studi di Firenze (University of Florence – Italy) where he graduated with honours (Cum Laude) in 2006.

His Specialist Orthopaedic Training began in the Orthopaedic and Trauma Centre of Florence, Italy (CTO Firenze 2006-2008) under the direction of Prof. Paolo Aglietti and Prof. Michele D’Ariezo and culminated in the University Hospital of Seville “Virgen Macarena” in 2014 (Spain). After these periods he underwent advance Surgical Training in England (UK) including working in World Leading Hospitals like St Mary’s Hospital in Paddington, London (Imperial College Healthcare NHS Trust) and The Jhon Radcliff and the Nuffield Orthopaedic Centre in Oxford (Oxford University Hospitals NHS Trust). This culminated with Advance Surgical Fellowships (Post CCT) in knee and hip arthroplasty (replacement) at the Southwest London Elective Orthopaedic Centre (SWELOC) and the very sought after - Post CCT fellowship in knee surgery at the Royal Berkshire Hospital NHS Foundation Trust, in Reading (Berkshire, England) where he was as a member of the Knee Unit, working alongside the very renown British Knee Surgeon Mr Sean O’Leary (FRCS), one of Surgeons who establish the (UK) National Ligament Registry in 2013 (He was Chairman from 2013-16 and remains on the Steering group – The UK National Ligament Registry is a national initiative to monitor and report on the outcomes of all ACL reconstructions in the UK -).

Before Settling in the Costa del Sol Area Dr. Poletti spend many years working as a Consultant Orthopaedic Hip and Knee Surgeon at the Saint Jhon of God Hospital of Tenerife (Canary IslandI) as well as short period at the Nykobing Falster University Hospital in Denmark, where he holds a Chief Physician Position (Knee Unit). He then went on to Work in Madrid, Spain for the Quiron Hospital Group including the Hospital Universitario Infanta Elena and Hospitales Quiron San Jose y Valle de Henares.
